Install it on your iPhone

Open this page in Safari on your iPhone, tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.

  1. Always open it from the home screen icon. That copy keeps its own data.
  2. It works offline once it has loaded.
  3. Everything is stored on the device. Export a backup from Settings now and then.

On a computer this preview uses the browser's own storage, separate from your phone.
